# September 15 Report Completion Plan

Date fixed: 2026-09-04

## Reporting Rule

The September report contains only complete experiments that pass their frozen
validation. An unfinished experiment is described in Future Work without
partial numerical findings. The later PLOS manuscript uses the NeurIPS paper as
its foundation, broadens the motivation and introduction, and incorporates the
full extensions after completion.

## Priority 1: Human-Agreement Experiment

Complete the primary forced-choice condition for all 740 decisive PoliProp
cases and all six judges. This experiment already has three complete judge
streams and is the most likely major extension to finish before the writing
cutoff. Regenerate the analysis only after every stream validates.

## Priority 2: Causal Matched-Length Experiment

Run a bounded causal verbosity study on a pre-specified subset of the validated
240 paper debates. Judge the original and matched-length counterfactual with the
same model panel under forced choice. Require semantic-preservation checks and
paired analysis. Length normalization is the report-priority intervention;
paired assertive-versus-cautious tone editing remains the next PLOS extension if
it cannot be completed and audited in time.

## Report Foundation

Retain the complete NeurIPS experiments and corrected analysis as the empirical
foundation. The report may add the two priorities above only after validation.
Detailed incomplete files, operational logs, and preliminary values do not
enter the results section.

## Work Deferred From the Deadline Path

- Full 840-debate six-model replication and its two 5,040-judgment stages.
- Broad repeated-prompt and candidate-order robustness on the new panel.
- Political-position generation, judging, and alignment models.
- Causal tone intervention if transformation auditing remains incomplete.

All completed rows from these studies remain versioned and resumable for the
PLOS manuscript. They are not deleted, overwritten, or mixed with the report's
validated datasets.
